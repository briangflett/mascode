Sent Email Log

Every outbound email CiviCRM recorded — one row per message, newest first. Covers Send Email, CiviMail bulk sends, automated lifecycle email and scheduled reminders. Sort any column; use the pager to change page size.

This lists what CiviCRM recorded, not what the mail relay delivered. Mail sent by a path that writes no activity leaves no trace here, and CiviCRM's outbound mail does not pass through the info@ mailbox, so that mailbox's Sent folder will not show these either.

For a bulk send, A recipient shows one recipient (alphabetically first, not first-addressed) and Recipients gives the total; per-recipient delivery, bounce and open detail lives in CiviMail's own reports.
